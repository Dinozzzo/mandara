About

The House of Mandara

Mandara is the result of more than seventy years of patience. From the volcanic groves of Valcura, on the lower slopes of Etna, to a small still passed down through four generations of the Ferrante family, every step in our process has been kept deliberately slow.

We grow what we use. We pick when the fruit decides — never before. We bottle in batches small enough to know each one. There are no shortcuts, no compromises in scale, no adjustments for fashion.

The result is a liqueur made the way it has always been made — quietly, honestly, and only by the family who started it.
