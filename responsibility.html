Responsibility

Mandara is made slowly, and it is meant to be enjoyed the same way. A great liqueur is not about how much is in the glass — it is about what surrounds it. The conversation, the company, the time it takes to settle.

Drink in moderation

We encourage everyone who chooses to enjoy Mandara to do so moderately. Pace yourself. Drink water between glasses. Eat alongside. Know your own limits and respect them. There is no version of a good evening that requires excess.

Never under the legal age

Mandara is intended only for adults of legal drinking age in their country of residence. We do not sell to, market to, or in any way encourage consumption by minors. If you are under the legal drinking age, please close this page and come back another time.

Never drink and drive

Alcohol impairs reaction time, judgement, and motor coordination — even in modest quantities. If you have been drinking, do not drive. Arrange a taxi, a ride-share, or public transport. A short delay is always better than a tragedy that cannot be undone.

If you are pregnant

Health authorities recommend abstaining from alcohol entirely during pregnancy. We follow that guidance and urge you to do the same.

Buying and gifting

When purchasing Mandara — whether for yourself or as a gift — ensure the recipient is of legal age and able to receive the bottle responsibly. Retailers and hospitality partners are expected to verify age at point of sale.

If alcohol is a concern

If you, or someone close to you, is struggling with alcohol, please reach out to a national support service in your country. Asking for help is the most considered choice anyone can make.
